Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-14
  Features:
    - Tiers: "New tier" on the editing bar copies the current tier into the next one; tier buttons switch between them.
    - Placed blueprints are tracked through their design marker, including rotation and flips.
    - Copies window per design: Show, Update (safe), Upgrade to next tier, Force match, plus Update all / Upgrade all.
    - Updates place ghosts for new entities, mark removed ones for deconstruction, and use upgrade orders for same-size replacements (e.g. stone to steel furnace).
    - Alt-mode label over each placed copy's marker showing design, tier and version.
    - Copying a placed copy (or a designer cell) into a new blueprint keeps it linked to its design.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-14
  Features:
    - Dedicated "Blueprint Designer" surface with one lab-tiled cell per design.
    - Edit a held blueprint (inventory or library) on the designer surface; saving writes it back to your cursor.
    - Ghosts placed on the designer surface are built instantly; deconstruction and upgrade marks apply instantly.
    - Design marker entity embedded in every saved blueprint so the mod can recognise its own designs.
    - Designs window listing all designs with Edit / Get blueprint / New.
